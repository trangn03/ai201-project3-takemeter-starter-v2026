analysis: states a claim where a specific, checkable fact (a stat, a date, a quote) is the
reason given for the claim — remove the fact and the claim stops holding up.
hot_take: states or clearly implies a confident verdict (including a rhetorical question
with an obvious implied answer) without a fact offered as the reason for it. A fact can be
present, but only decoratively — the verdict doesn't depend on it.
reaction: describes a scene, feeling, or event with no verdict stated or implied. If you
can't point to a sentence that asserts "X is true/should happen," it's reaction, even if a
number appears in the text.
