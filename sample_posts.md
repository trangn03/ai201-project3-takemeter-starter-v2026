## Takeaways for taxonomy design

- **Flair isn't the taxonomy.** Reddit's own flairs (News, Quotes, Media, Opinion Piece,
  Stats, Match Clip, Goal Clip, Official Source) describe *source/format*, not the kind
  of thing worth classifying for this assignment (e.g. tone, argument quality, effort).
- **Submission bodies are mostly empty.** ~30 of 45 have no selftext — the title is the
  entire post. Real variation in length/voice/argument shows up in: transcribed quotes
  (#7, #9, #24), the one OC analysis (#23), OP editorializing (#33), official statements
  (#32), and human-interest framing (#26, #40). Comments on these threads are where the
  bulk of "real" quality variance (effortful breakdown vs. one-word reaction vs. banter)
  will actually live — worth pulling comment threads, not just submissions, for the 200.
- **Topic concentration.** This snapshot is dominated by two live storylines (Man City
  verdict, Ronaldo NT exit) — a time-of-day sampling artifact, not representative of the
  sub's normal spread. Collecting across multiple days/threads will matter for taxonomy
  balance (the `check_dataset` 70%-dominance check will catch this if ignored).
